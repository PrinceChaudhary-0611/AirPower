About Us

Excellence in Industrial Gas Manufacturing

With decades of experience, cutting-edge technology, and a commitment to innovation, we serve a wide range of industries, including healthcare, metallurgy, food processing, electronics, pharmaceuticals, and petrochemicals. Our mission is to provide top-quality gas solutions that drive efficiency, sustainability, and safety for our clients worldwide.

Our Products: Innovation and Precision at Their Best.

At Air Power, we take pride in manufacturing a wide range of industrial gases and specialized equipment tailored to meet the diverse needs of various industries. Our key products include:

1. Oxygen (O₂) Manufacturing. Oxygen is a vital industrial gas used in numerous applications, from medical and healthcare industries to metal cutting, welding, and chemical processes. Our high-purity oxygen ensures superior performance and reliability, meeting international standards of safety and quality.

2. Nitrogen (N₂) Manufacturing. Nitrogen plays a crucial role in industries such as food preservation, electronics manufacturing, and pharmaceuticals. Our nitrogen production facilities utilize cutting-edge technology to deliver ultra-pure nitrogen that meets stringent industry requirements.

3. Hydrogen (H₂) Manufacturing Hydrogen is a clean energy source with diverse applications, including chemical manufacturing, fuel cells, and industrial refining. Our hydrogen production technology ensures high efficiency, purity, and sustainability, making us a trusted supplier in the market.

4. Ammonia Cracker Units. Ammonia crackers are essential for producing hydrogen-rich gas mixtures used in heat treatment and metallurgical applications. Our ammonia cracking units are designed for optimal efficiency, durability, and performance, ensuring consistent output with minimal maintenance.

5. IHR Dryer Machines.

Our IHR (Indirect Heat Regenerative) Dryer Machines are engineered to provide superior drying solutions for various industrial applications. These dryers are energy-efficient, robust, and designed to handle demanding operational conditions, ensuring longevity and reliability.
